Derived assets

assets/sound/drip-single.wav is a 0.62-second cut of assets_sound_src/drip.wav,
trimmed, faded and normalised for use as the Morse drip. It is a derivative of
that recording and carries its source's licence and attribution.

assets/sound/plate-scene-5.m4a is a 6-second excerpt of its source recording,
trimmed and faded for use as the Scene 5 plate sting. Same licence and
attribution as the full recording.

assets/sound/bed-scene-2.m4a is also played at 0.89 speed as Scene 6's bed. No
separate file, and no separate credit.

Scene 4's low sustained note is synthesised at runtime with WebAudio and is not
a sourced asset.
